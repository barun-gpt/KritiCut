Quick guide

Everything you need to make your first cut

Start

  • New project — pick a template, then import media.
  • Import — video, photos and audio; tap a thumbnail to add it.
  • Aspect — choose 9:16, 16:9, 1:1 or 4:5 from the top bar.

Timeline

  • Tap a clip to select, drag it to move, drag the edges to trim.
  • Drag the ruler to scrub; the playhead marks the current frame.
  • Split cuts every clip under the playhead.

Look & motion

  • Filters and Adjust apply to the selected clip.
  • Keys animates position, scale, rotation and opacity over time.
  • Text can be dragged straight on the preview.

Shortcuts

Space play / pause S split Del delete clip ←→ step frame Ctrl+Z undo Ctrl+S save draft Esc deselect

Tip: your draft autosaves after every change.
